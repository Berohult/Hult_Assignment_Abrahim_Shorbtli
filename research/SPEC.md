# BUILD SPEC

## Design direction
"Majlis at night" — a dark, editorial, Arabic-first luxury system. Base palette: near-black espresso/charcoal (#171310 family) with warm off-black cards, one brushed-gold accent used only as 1px hairlines, price chips and badges, and a Diriyah-Tan sand/mudbrick secondary (#C8A882 family) for section tints and skeleton placeholders. No gold-on-black glitz, no arabesque wallpaper — negative space IS the luxury signal (Noma/kaiseki restraint), with exactly ONE maximal moment: the cafe's Arabic wordmark as a large calligraphic SVG hero that reveals letter-by-letter (word-mask, not per-glyph) like coffee pouring. Typography: two families max, both covering AR+Latin — display = Noto Kufi Arabic (geometric Kufic, the current GCC luxury register per Balenciaga/29LT and UAE Design System 2.0), body = IBM Plex Sans Arabic; Arabic set 1.1-1.2x larger than Latin, line-height 1.75, letter-spacing forced to 0 on all Arabic. Document root is dir="rtl" lang="ar" by default; all layout via CSS logical properties so the EN toggle atomically flips direction, metrics, and content in one 250ms crossfade. Heritage abstracted, not decorated: Najdi arch shapes as image masks on hero photos, a single-line abstracted mashrabiya SVG as section dividers, faint oversized Kufic letterforms at 4-6% opacity as chapter watermarks. Photography: dark side-lit moody food shots (latte art, dates on stone) — full-bleed arch-masked heroes for signatures only, small uniform thumbnails or type-only rows for basics; every image sits on a dominant-color LQIP so nothing ever flashes white. Motion vocabulary is exactly three named moves used everywhere: MORPH (card photo → detail bottom-sheet continuity via View Transitions with fade fallback), RISE (16px stagger fade-up on scroll entrance, CSS scroll-driven with IntersectionObserver fallback), SQUISH (scale 0.96 spring on every pressable). Frosted-glass (backdrop-filter) sticky header and bottom order bar, warm-tinted. Prices are bare round numerals (24, not SAR 24.00) with the new Saudi Riyal symbol (U+20C1) as a small inline SVG. Mood in one line: % Arabica's Kyoto restraint wearing a Najdi arch, photographed after Isha.

## Wow in 30 seconds (ranked)
1. 1. The scan itself: QR → fully painted Arabic menu in under 1 second on 4G (single self-contained HTML, <14KB critical payload, LQIP placeholders) — felt viscerally against the owner's own 8-second PDF in a side-by-side scan

3. 3. The language toggle flip: one tap on the العربية/English pill and the entire layout mirrors — chips reverse scroll direction, prices re-anchor, type metrics swap — in one smooth 250ms transition; hand the owner the phone mid-scroll and let them do it
4. 4. The Signature hero: full-bleed arch-masked 'Saffron Gold Latte' card (Emirates Palace playbook) with a 3-second steam micro-loop — 'that's MY menu?' moment
5. 5. Tap a card → photo morphs into an iOS-style draggable bottom sheet with description, calories, caffeine mg, allergens, and priced modifiers — reads as a native app, not a webpage
6. 6. Add to order → thumbnail arcs into the frosted bottom bar, count bounces, live SAR total ticks up (Uobei live-spend pattern) — the owner sees average-ticket machinery, not decoration
7. 7. 'Order via WhatsApp' composes a structured bilingual order message with items, modifiers, table number and total — a static site that visibly takes orders, commission-free
8. 8. SFDA compliance spotted: kcal on every item, caffeine mg with the 400mg footnote, salt icon, burn-it-off walk icon — 'this vendor knows my market' without a word said
9. 9. Provenance bean cards: 'Jazan Khawlani — 1,800 m — UNESCO Heritage' with tasting-note chips and cup score — instantly reads specialty-native, out-designing Redro/Myazu
10. 10. Sticky category chip bar with a gliding gold active pill auto-centering as you flick-scroll 60fps through 8 chapters — the app-feel navigation spine

## Must have
- **Arabic-first true RTL with atomic AR/EN toggle: dir=rtl lang=ar on <html> as default, all strings in one {ar,en} JS object, CSS logical properties throughout, per-locale type metrics via root variables, <bdi> auto-wrapping of Latin tokens and all prices/numbers, flag-free labeled pill toggle persisted in localStorage, 250ms crossfade on switch** — The single most visible differentiator to a native-speaking owner — competitors ship broken machine-mirrored Arabic; a flawless flip demo IS the close ('designed in Arabic first' is a positioning line no template vendor can claim)
- **Instant-load architecture: one self-contained HTML file, critical CSS inlined under 14KB compressed, menu data embedded (no fetch), two preloaded subsetted WOFF2 fonts (AR display + AR/Latin body, ~60-80KB each, full Arabic block intact, font-display:swap with metric-matched fallbacks), AVIF→WebP images at exact display size, dominant-color LQIP inlined per dish, fetchpriority=high on the one hero, loading=lazy below fold, content-visibility:auto per section, total first view <500KB, LCP <1s on throttled 4G, CLS 0.00** — Speed is the first impression before any design is seen and the most theatrical sales weapon (scan their PDF vs ours); every other wow depends on it landing before attention lapses
- **Sticky frosted-glass header + horizontally scrollable category chip bar with scroll-spy: active gold pill glides between chips (transform only), auto-centers on scroll, smooth-scroll anchors, mirrors correctly in RTL; header holds wordmark, language pill at inline-start, WhatsApp icon at inline-end** — The highest-leverage QR-menu UX pattern per every UX source — it's the navigation spine that makes the page feel like an app, and its RTL-correct reversal is itself a demo moment
- **Chaptered menu IA with heritage storytelling: 8 named chapters with one-line poetic bilingual intros (not generic categories), opening with a 'قهوتنا | Our Qahwa' hero block — dallah line-icon, UNESCO Intangible Heritage note, 'served with Sukkari dates' — and a two-line founder-story block ('قصتنا / Our Story') ready to personalize live per prospect** — Trèsind/Dishoom/spa-menu grammar converts a price list into an authored work; the founder-story block personalized in-pitch is the highest-emotion closing moment, and qahwa heritage carries official national pride
- **SFDA-compliant metadata line on every item, styled as fine-dining refinement: kcal on all items, caffeine mg on beverages with the 400mg/day footnote, salt-shaker icon on the high-sodium item, walk-to-burn icon on desserts, nine-allergen glyphs (nuts/dairy/gluten/egg/sesame...), one quiet venue-level halal line in the footer, VAT-inclusive note ('جميع الأسعار تشمل الضريبة 15%')** — Legally required in Saudi since July 2025 and owners are actively anxious about inspections — compliance-by-default is the 'this vendor knows my market' signal that beats any feature pitch, delivered as small-caps elegance rather than clutter
- **Simulated ordering loop with WhatsApp close: tap-to-add with fly-to-bar animation, persistent frosted bottom bar showing item count + live SAR total with tick animation, cart sheet with one 'Complete your order' dessert suggestion, primary CTA 'اطلب عبر واتساب / Order via WhatsApp' composing a structured wa.me message (items, modifiers, table no., total, in the active language), followed by a simulated status strip (Received → Preparing → On the way) and quick actions (Request bill / Call waiter / Rate us on Google)** — Turns a static site into a functioning commission-free ordering system in the owner's eyes (the #1 economic pain vs 15-30% delivery-app cuts), demonstrates ticket-raising machinery, and dovetails with Menu Sadah's own WhatsApp-concierge brand
- **Item detail as iOS-style draggable bottom sheet with photo MORPH continuity: shared-element View Transition from card thumbnail to sheet hero (feature-checked, fade fallback), grabber handle, drag-to-dismiss with velocity, background scales to 0.95; sheet contains description, metadata, Ichiran-style bilingual customization chips (sweetness %, milk type, ice, saffron/cardamom intensity) and priced add-ons (extra shot +3, oat milk +2, saffron cream +4)** — The bottom sheet + morph is THE native-food-app signature that makes users read the page as an installed app; priced contextual modifiers are the proven Toast upsell mechanic and the Ichiran zero-conversation customization story
- **Menu-psychology engineering baked visibly in: bare round numerals with small Riyal-symbol SVG (no price columns, no leader lines, price nested after description), one premium anchor first in each category (Reserve Gesha 65 / Dallah Pot 42), strict badge budget of one 'الأكثر طلبًا' + one 'اختيار الشيف' gold pill per category, 5-7 items per category, high-margin items at first/last slots, one bundle card with struck-through anchor math ('52 → 45')** — Each choice carries a citable number (8.15% no-currency, +10.2% anchors, 30% single-highlight, 27% descriptive labels) that becomes the pitch script — the demo doubles as a live menu-engineering lesson that justifies $49/month as engineering-as-a-service
- **Provenance bean cards + Reserve block: each pour-over gets origin flag, farm, altitude byline ('Haraz Terraces, Yemen — 2,200 m'), process, cup score, roast-date line, and 3 tasting-note chips; section ordered ascending by altitude with a thin connecting rule; a gold-accented 'Reserve | محصول محدود' block (Panama Gesha, Yemenia auction lot, 'only 6 cups daily')** — The most specialty-native component possible — speaks the exact register Camel Step/Brew92/Onyx owners obsess over, flatters Saudi roasters' auction dominance, and functions as the price anchor; directly out-designs the Redro/Myazu benchmark
- **Three-move motion system with hard performance budget: MORPH / RISE (staggered 16px scroll entrances, 60-80ms apart) / SQUISH (scale 0.96 tap feedback everywhere), one shared easing pair, compositor-tier properties only (transform/opacity), total motion JS under ~5KB + one IntersectionObserver + ~40 lines for the sheet, full prefers-reduced-motion block swapping everything to fades, tested on mid-range Android** — Award-level polish comes from a small consistent vocabulary at unbroken 60fps, not spectacle — one dropped frame destroys the luxury illusion, and consistency across 60 cards is what reads as engineered rather than templated
- **Tiered photography with 'pairs beautifully with' cross-sell chips: full-bleed arch-masked hero photos on 6-8 signatures and all desserts, uniform small thumbnails elsewhere, type-only rows for basics; under key items a tappable 'يُقدَّم مع / Pairs with' chip that jumps to the paired item (Spanish latte → pistachio kunafa)** — Resolves the photos-everywhere vs restraint conflict: selective heroes preserve premium perception while capturing the +30% photo lift on margin items, and the pairing chip is FineDine's top-paywall feature reproduced free — a demo beat that visibly raises tickets
- **Owner-facing pitch page (separate route) built to close in one WhatsApp thread: fear-first headline, side-by-side PDF-vs-demo visual, SAR-first ROI math, 4-column price comparison anchored on Foodics/WowMenu, mock analytics dashboard, mock WhatsApp edit conversation, styled branded QR on an acrylic-stand mock, zero forms — every CTA a prefilled wa.me deep link** — The demo is the pitch (Toast/Mr Yum playbook) but the offer still needs an artifact that converts fence-sitters and survives being forwarded to a partner/brother — and forms would kill the 5-minute speed-to-lead advantage

## Anti-patterns
- Never a PDF or any pinch-zoom layout — the entire category exists against it
- No price columns, leader-line dots, charm pricing (18.99), or 'SAR 24.00' formatting — bare round numerals with a quiet symbol
- No flags on the language toggle, and never auto-redirect by locale without a visible toggle
- No per-item HALAL badges (signals doubt — one quiet venue-level line instead), no pork/alcohol content, and never the word 'mocktail'
- No letter-spacing on Arabic, no character-level Arabic font subsetting, no blanket scaleX(-1) mirroring (symbolic icons, logos, numbers, and scrollbar stay unmirrored), no English-first-then-translate workflow, no lorem ipsum
- No badge spam — one Bestseller + one Chef's Pick per category, ever; over-badging turns signal to noise
- No photo on every item — mixed tiering is deliberate; photo-on-everything reads delivery-app cheap
- No animation libraries, WebGL, GSAP, or framework — motion budget is ~5KB of vanilla JS + CSS; no width/height/top/left animations, no mousemove parallax, and always a prefers-reduced-motion block
- No app install, signup, cookie wall, form, or interstitial anywhere between scan and menu — and no forms on the pitch page either (WhatsApp only)
- No external CDN fonts/scripts, no menu data fetched from an API — everything self-hosted and inlined for GitHub Pages
- No gold-on-black arabesque glitz, busy pattern backgrounds, or souvenir-shop calligraphy everywhere — one calligraphic gesture, Swiss-clean everything else
- No fabricated testimonials, fake client logos, or invented competitor claims on the pitch page — borrowed category stats with real attributions only
- No 'Arabic Coffee' wording — it is 'Saudi coffee / قهوة سعودية' (2022 national campaign), and no machine-translated Arabic anywhere
- No lazy-loading the LCP hero image, no gray skeleton boxes (brand-tinted LQIP instead), no full-screen backdrop-filter surfaces on cheap Androids
- Don't hedge the design between minimal and maximal — commit to Noma-calm body + one dramatic hero moment; hedging is what templates do
